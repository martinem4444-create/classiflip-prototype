About us

Classified Marketplace | Local Buy & Sell · Mississauga, Ontario

ClassiFlip is a local classifieds marketplace built in Mississauga, for Mississauga first — and then for the rest of Canada. Browse free, post with an account, message sellers directly, and boost the ads that matter. No noise, no clutter, just neighbours buying and selling.

Built lean, built honest

ClassiFlip started in a basement home office — no office tower, no sales team, no outside funding. The founder, Martin Mestre, built it together with Meta Muse, an AI co-builder: Martin bringing twenty-plus years of selling, a lifetime in Mississauga, and the eye of someone who has actually bought and sold his whole life; Muse bringing the code, fast, in the same room, day after day.

That is the whole team. Two builders, one old computer, and a simple rule: keep overhead at zero, keep the site clean, and let the money from real sales fund whatever comes next — never the other way around.

Meet the founder

Martin E. Mestre — founder of ClassiFlip. Twenty-plus years of selling, a lifetime in Mississauga, and a simple mission: give his neighbours a clean, honest place to buy and sell locally. Don't just sell it. Flip it.

What we believe

  • Local first. A marketplace should feel like your neighbourhood, not a warehouse. Mississauga first, the GTA next, Canada after that.
  • Clean over cluttered. No ads in your private messages. No bombardment. If a placement would annoy a buyer, it does not belong on the site.
  • Give first. Browsing is free. Private sellers post free. Businesses pay only for the volume and placement they actually use — and founding advertisers lock a fair rate for twelve months.
  • Humans decide. Every ad passes through a human approval queue before it goes live. If an ad is rejected, the seller is told exactly why, and exactly how to fix it.

What ClassiFlip is for

For the person clearing out a garage, the dealer moving inventory, the agent listing a home, the tradesperson finding their next job, and the neighbour who would rather buy from someone down the street than from a warehouse across the country. Don't just sell it. Flip it — on ClassiFlip.

Contact: email only at launch — hello@classiflip.com. We read every message, and we answer as people, not as a ticket queue.

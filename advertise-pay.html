How it works

Three steps, no account with Stripe needed.

1
Pick your package
Choose the plan that fits your business above and hit Pay securely.
2
Pay on Stripe's secure checkout
Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on Stripe's own checkout page. Your receipt is emailed to you automatically.
3
Your ad goes live

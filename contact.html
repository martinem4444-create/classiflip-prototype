Contact us

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · ClassiFlip, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

ClassiFlip is run by real people in Mississauga — every message is read by Martin, the founder. The fastest way to reach us is email.

Email us

hello@classiflip.com

We reply within one business day in most cases — usually much faster.

To get help fastest, include:

  • The email address on your ClassiFlip account
  • Your order number (for example CF-1001) or the title of the ad you're asking about
  • A short note on what happened and what you need

Businesses & advertising

Questions about packages, founding spots, or boosts? Email hello@classiflip.com with "Advertising" in the subject line, or see the Advertise page for packages and prices.

Something wrong with an ad?

The fastest route is the Report button on the ad itself. When three different people report the same ad, it is automatically paused until we review it. For anything urgent, report it and email us as well.

Please never send us your password or banking details — we will never ask for them. ClassiFlip support is by email only.

Help & FAQ

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · ClassiFlip, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

Posting an ad

Create a free account, tap Post an ad, add your photos (you can pick them all at once), fill in the details, and submit. Every ad is reviewed by a real person before it goes live — you'll get an email the moment it's live, or an email explaining what to fix if it needs a change.

Editing your ad

Changing only the price? Your ad stays live and the new price shows right away — and anyone who saved your ad gets a price-drop email if the price went down. Changing anything else sends the ad back for a quick review first.

Packages for businesses

Car dealers, real estate agents, landlords, service businesses, and employers post with a package from the Advertise page. Reserve with Interac e-Transfer and your package is activated within 24 hours of us confirming your transfer — card payment is coming. Founding packages are 50% off their category's regular price: first month free, a 3-month minimum term, your price locked for 12 months, and 20% off when you renew after 12 paid months.

Boosts

A boost costs $2.99 and pins your ad to the top of its category for 7 days. You'll find the Boost button on your ad in My Ads.

Saving ads & price drops

Tap the heart on any ad to save it. If the price drops, we'll email you the new price — with the percentage saved.

Messages

Buyers and sellers chat right on ClassiFlip. For privacy, chats show first names only — no full names, no email addresses.

Sold something?

Mark it sold from My Ads. It will appear in Recently Sold on the homepage — good proof for your store.

Forgot your password?

Use the reset link on the sign-in page and we'll email you a fresh one. Still stuck? Contact us.

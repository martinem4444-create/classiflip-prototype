Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · ClassiFlip, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

ClassiFlip is a local classifieds marketplace. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why we collect it, and what we never share. If you have a question about it, contact us at hello@classiflip.com (email only at launch).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password (stored securely). You must be 18 or older to sell on ClassiFlip.
  • Listings you post: title, price, category, condition, neighbourhood or city, description, tags, and photos you upload. Listing photos must show items only, not people.
  • Messages: messages you send to other users about a listing, so both sides can complete a deal.
  • Orders and boosts: business name, package, amount, and payment reference (for example a CF order code or BST boost code) so we can match a payment to a package.
  • Waitlist emails: if you join the waitlist, we keep only your email address, to tell you when ClassiFlip launches. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

What we do NOT collect or share

  • We never ask for, and never publish, your street address as a private seller. Your listing location is a neighbourhood or city only.
  • We never publish your personal phone number or personal email address. Businesses may choose to publish a business phone number and business address on their own storefront page — only what they typed themselves.
  • We never sell your personal information to anyone, and we never use it for advertising to third parties.
  • Payment card numbers are never seen or stored by ClassiFlip. Card payments go through Stripe's own secure checkout; Interac e-Transfers go through your bank.

How we use what we collect

Only to run the marketplace: show your listings, deliver messages, approve ads, activate packages and boosts, send admin alerts about pending ads, and tell you when a package or boost goes live. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

Who can see what

  • Public: your listings, your display name, and — for businesses only — the storefront details that business published itself.
  • Private: your email, your account details, and your messages. Only you, the other person in a conversation, and ClassiFlip's admin can see a message thread.
  • Admin only: listing, user, order, and boost totals. Totals are never published publicly.

Photos, cookies, and tracking

Listing photos are stored so buyers can see what you are selling. We use only the basic storage needed to keep you signed in and to remember your theme and saved listings on your own device. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your own ads from your Dashboard at any time (pending ads can be deleted; live ads follow the rules shown there).
  • Ask us to delete your account or your waitlist email by writing to hello@classiflip.com.
  • Businesses can edit or remove their published storefront details from their Dashboard.
Plain-language promise: ClassiFlip is built lean and honest from a home office in Mississauga. Your information stays yours, it stays in Canada's marketplace, and it is used only to help you buy and sell locally. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

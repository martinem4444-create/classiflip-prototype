Refund Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · ClassiFlip, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

ClassiFlip is a local classifieds marketplace. This policy explains, in plain language, when money comes back and when it does not. The short version: firm when value was delivered, fair when it was not. Questions go to hello@classiflip.com.

Packages

  • Activated packages are not refundable. Once a package is activated and the first ad under it has been posted, the package fee is earned. This applies to founding, regular, and term packages.
  • 48-hour cooling-off. Within 48 hours of payment, if no ad has been posted under the package yet, you can ask for a full refund — no questions asked.
  • Not activated yet? Full refund. If you paid for a package and it has not been activated and no ad has been posted under it, you can cancel for a full refund to the original payment method.

Boosts ($2.99 / 7 days)

  • A boost is not refundable once it has been activated.
  • If a boost was paid for but never activated — because the ad was rejected in moderation or because of a ClassiFlip error — the $2.99 is refunded or credited toward a future boost. Your choice.

If we pause your package

If ClassiFlip pauses your package or a paid ad (for example, a moderation pause), your clock extends by the number of days paused. You never lose paid time because of a pause on our side.

Rejected ads

Every ad is reviewed before it goes live. If your ad is rejected, we tell you why and how to fix it, and you can repost. If you choose not to repost, your package stays credited to your account for a future ad — it is not refunded in cash.

Our mistakes are on us

Duplicate charges, a payment taken for a package that was never delivered, or a ClassiFlip outage that takes a paid ad offline for more than 24 hours are fixed with a credit or a refund — your choice on duplicate charges.

Term plans

On a discounted term plan, leaving early means settling up the discounts received up to the exit date. No other early-exit fee applies.

How to ask

Email hello@classiflip.com with your order number (CF- for packages and orders, BST- for boosts). We decide within 5 business days, and approved refunds go back to the original payment method.

Plain-language promise: ClassiFlip is built lean and honest from a home office in Mississauga. We keep fees we have earned, we return fees we have not, and if the mistake is ours, we make it right. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

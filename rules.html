Posting rules

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · ClassiFlip, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

These rules keep ClassiFlip fair, honest, and worth browsing. Every ad is reviewed by a real person before it goes live, and ads that break the rules can be paused or removed.

The basics

  • One ad per item — no duplicates or reposting the same item in several categories.
  • Use the right category, an honest title, and your own real photos of the actual item.
  • Price honestly. If it's free, list it in Free — a $0 price shows as FREE.
  • No keyword stuffing, misleading claims, or bait pricing ("$1" ads for items that cost more).
  • You must be 18 or older to post.

Never allowed

  • Illegal, stolen, counterfeit, or recalled-and-unsafe items
  • Weapons, ammunition, and fireworks
  • Drugs, drug paraphernalia, tobacco, and vaping products
  • Alcohol
  • Adult services or escort ads
  • Live animals (not a launch category)
  • Scams, pyramid schemes, and "work from home" recruiting that charges a fee
  • Anyone else's personal information

Businesses

  • Dealers, agents, landlords, service businesses, and employers post with a package — see Advertise.
  • Inventory posted under a business must belong to that business.
  • Your store name and ads must not impersonate another business.

How moderation works

  • Every new ad is reviewed before it goes live.
  • Price-only edits stay live instantly; other edits are reviewed again.
  • Three reports from three different people automatically pause an ad until we review it.
  • Repeat rule-breakers can lose posting privileges or their account.

Disagree with a decision? Contact us — a real person will take another look.

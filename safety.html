Safety tips

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · ClassiFlip, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

ClassiFlip is built for local, in-person deals. A few simple habits keep almost every trade safe:

Meet smart

  • Meet in a public, busy place in daylight whenever you can — many Peel Regional Police stations offer safe-exchange parking spots.
  • Bring a friend or family member for large items or high-value deals.
  • Tell someone where you're going and when you expect to be back.

See it before you pay

  • Inspect the item in person before money changes hands.
  • Pay at the meetup — cash, or Interac e-Transfer while you're standing together.
  • Never wire money, send gift cards, or e-transfer a deposit to someone you haven't met.
  • Beware the classic scams: "buyers" who want to ship, send a courier, or overpay by cheque and ask for the difference back. That's not a sale — walk away.

Protect your information

  • Keep your conversation in ClassiFlip messages until you're ready to meet.
  • Never share banking details, passwords, or ID photos with a buyer or seller.
  • ClassiFlip chats show first names only — keep it that way until the meetup.

Buying a vehicle?

  • Ask for the ownership and check the VIN matches the vehicle.
  • Meet the seller at the vehicle, in daylight, and take a proper look and test drive.
  • Get a used-vehicle information package where required, and complete the transfer at ServiceOntario.

If something feels off

Trust your gut — a deal you can walk away from is never a loss. Use the Report button on any ad: three reports from different people automatically pause the ad until we review it. For anything serious, contact us as well.

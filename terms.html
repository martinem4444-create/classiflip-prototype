What ClassiFlip is

Ads must follow the Posting rules. Every ad is reviewed before it goes live. We may pause, edit for rule compliance, or remove any ad, and we may suspend accounts that break the rules repeatedly. When three different users report an ad, it is automatically paused until we review it.

Packages, boosts, and payment

  • Business packages are billed monthly at the prices shown on the Advertise page.
  • Founding packages: 50% off the category's regular price, first month free, a minimum term of 3 months, and your price locked for 12 months. After 12 paid months, renewals get 20% off.
  • If a founding package is cancelled during the minimum term, the value of the free first month becomes payable — see the Refund policy.
  • Interac e-Transfer reserves are activated within 24 hours of us confirming your transfer.
  • A boost costs $2.99 and pins an ad to the top of its category for 7 days. Boost time runs from activation and isn't paused if an ad is later removed for breaking the rules.
  • Refunds are handled under the Refund policy.
